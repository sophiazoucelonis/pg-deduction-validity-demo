Deduction Validity App — Complete Solution

Primary Workflow: Remittance → Reconciliation → Deduction Coding → SAP Posting

1
Remittance received in AR Claims inbox
Status: Complete | Duration: Instant
A customer sends payment via email to the AR Claims inbox. This is the trigger that starts the entire workflow.
Trigger / Outlook
↓
2
Read email + attachments via MLWB
Status: Complete | Duration: 0.8s
Machine Learning Workbench extracts text, numbers, and structured data from the remittance email and any PDF attachments.
AI / Celonis
↓
3
Archive documents to SharePoint and Operational View
Status: Complete | Duration: Instant
Original documents are filed for audit, compliance, and regulatory purposes. Documents are linked to the invoice in the Operational View.
System / SharePoint
↓
4
Ingest invoice data from SAP
Status: Complete | Duration: 1.2s
Pull the open invoice record from SAP including amount, due date, payment terms, and all line-item details.
System / SAP
↓
5
Reconcile payment against open invoice
Status: Complete | Duration: 1.2s
Compare the payment received (from the remittance) with the invoice amount owed. This is where short-pays and overpayments surface.
System / Celonis
↓
6
Fetch pricing sheet and POD data from trackers
Status: Complete | Duration: 2.0s
If amounts don't match, retrieve the pricing tracker from SharePoint and check the Proof of Delivery (POD) status to understand why the gap exists.
System / SharePoint
↓
7
Determine reason code — D10 / C30 / C50 / A70 / D20
Status: Complete | Duration: 2.4s
The AI system decides which reason code applies:
  • D10: Returns (customer returned goods)
  • C30: Pricing difference (price variance detected)
  • C50: Other deduction type
  • A70: Valid payment (clean POD, no issues)
  • D20: Pending POD (awaiting proof of delivery)
AI / Celonis
↓
8
Recommend deduction valid or invalid
Status: Current
The AI agent validates the deduction: Is this a legitimate deduction or potential revenue leakage? Flags suspicious patterns for manual review.
Agent / Celonis
↓
9
Post cash application and deduction to SAP
Status: Pending | Duration: 1.6s
Write the decision back to SAP: post the cash application and the deduction with the determined reason code. Updates open item records.
System / SAP
↓
10
Email hand-off to CMO / PS action owners
Status: Pending
Notify the CMO (Chief Merchant Operations) or PS (Professional Services) team if manual action is needed — e.g., follow up on a return or investigate a pricing gap.
System / Outlook
↓
11
Send FBL5 report to AR team
Status: Pending
Daily FBL5 report (SAP open items list) is sent to the AR team showing all new postings and deductions from today's runs. Provides full audit trail and reconciliation.
System / Outlook
